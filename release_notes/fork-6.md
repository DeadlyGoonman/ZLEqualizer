## Fork build 6

**New**
- Ghost curve glow point is colourable (orange by default): Settings > Colour > "Ghost Glow Colour".
- Ctrl+A selects all active bands.
- Graph Q wheel direction inverted: scroll up now narrows a bell, scroll down widens it.

**Fixed**
- Clicking artifact when scrolling a high/low pass changes its slope (dB/oct). Rapid wheel steps now crossfade into each other, and the Parallel structure fades too.

**Install**
- Run `ZLEqualizer-fork-6-windows-installer.exe` (installs to `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3`), or unzip the VST3 by hand. Close your DAW first. Installed plugins also offer this update automatically.
